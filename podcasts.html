Conversations That Heal.

Creative Arts Therapy Conversations — hosted by Dr. Peter Jampel, President of Baltic Street Into Action. Expert conversations with leading music, drama, poetry, and art therapists about what recovery through the arts really looks like.

Music, Family, and the Roots of Recovery

Music on the Inside: Reentry Through Mentorship

How the organization Music on the Inside (MOTI) promotes self-esteem and improves community reentry through musical mentorships with professional musicians, during and after incarceration at Rikers Island.

Peer Arts Training: The Power of Shared Experience

Mental health career development for people with lived experience, and how the arts offer an area of specialized training at the Howie the Harp Peer Training Program, supported by a BSIA grant.

Episode 08

August 14, 2024
Episode 09

Dr. John Mondanaro: Music at End of Life

Dr. John Mondanaro, Director of Expressive Arts Therapies, Department of Geriatrics and Palliative Medicine, Icahn School of Medicine, on music therapy and palliative care.

August 22, 2024

Dr. Connie Tomaino: Music, Memory, and the Brain

September 4, 2024
Episode 11
